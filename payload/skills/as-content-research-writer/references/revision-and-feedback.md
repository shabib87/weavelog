# Revision And Feedback

Use this reference when the user wants section-level critique or targeted rewrites.

## Review Priorities

- clarity of the section's job
- strength of the hook or opening sentence
- visibility of the mechanism behind the claim
- visibility of the consequence or implication
- evidence and specificity
- continuity with the surrounding sections
- sentence-level drag, repetition, or abstraction
- argument density

## Feedback Style

- lead with the most important issue when it is obvious
- note strengths briefly when they materially affect the revision
- keep revision notes concrete and local to the text
- explain why a change helps, not only what to change
- prefer direct rewrites or structural fixes over vague encouragement
- preserve the author's voice unless a different tone is requested

## Output Pattern

- main issue
- what works
- what is weak or missing
- suggested rewrite or structural adjustment
- next step if another skill should take over
