# Outline And Structure

Use this reference when the user has a topic, rough outline, or partial draft that needs stronger shape.

## Focus

- clarify the working thesis
- identify the audience and the promise to the reader
- sequence sections so the draft moves through problem, mechanism, and consequence
- mark where evidence, examples, or transitions are missing
- mark where mechanism or implication is missing even when the topic is clear

## Helpful Moves

- turn vague headings into claim-bearing headings
- turn symptom-only sections into mechanism questions
- cut duplicate sections before polishing wording
- name the gap if the draft jumps from setup to conclusion too quickly
- cut tutorial steps that distract from the real argument
- prefer one clear throughline over several half-developed ones

## Output Pattern

- working thesis
- proposed structure or revised outline
- mechanism and consequence gaps
- gaps to fill
- suggested next step
