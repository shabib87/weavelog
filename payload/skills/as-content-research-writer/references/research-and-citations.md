# Research And Citations

Use this reference when the draft needs support from external sources.

## Source Discipline

- prefer primary sources first
- use secondary summaries only when they materially help and are clearly labeled
- distinguish confirmed facts from proposed wording
- mark unsupported claims instead of papering over them

## Citation Rules

- never invent citations, quotes, dates, or numbers
- keep links, titles, authors, and dates attached to the notes when available
- call out when a claim still needs verification rather than presenting it as settled

## Output Pattern

- claim or question being researched
- source-backed finding
- citation or link
- unresolved questions or follow-up checks
