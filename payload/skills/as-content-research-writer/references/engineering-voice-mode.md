# Engineering Voice Mode

Use this reference when the writing task is an insight-led engineering post, essay, or section for
experienced engineers.

For blog work, keep this condensed profile aligned with the repo-owned editorial voice contract
rather than letting it drift into a separate style.

## Default Posture

- conversational, direct, and grounded in real systems
- plain language over polished technical-writing tone
- insight over tutorial
- authority through observation and reasoning, not role labels

## Default Shape

- start from a real observation, tension, or failure
- explain the mechanism behind it
- show the consequence or broader implication
- end with a practical takeaway, heuristic, or reframing

## Helpful Editing Moves

- turn generic headings into claim-bearing headings
- replace abstract claims with concrete system behavior
- cut tutorial padding when the real value is judgment
- tighten paragraphs until each sentence earns its place
- rewrite weak endings into sharper consequences or decision rules

## Avoid

- marketing language
- generic thought-leadership phrasing
- fake balance or softened criticism
- self-promotional role signaling
- vague claims with no failure mode or consequence
